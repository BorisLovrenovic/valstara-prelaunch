Why we exist

Most people who buy digital goods below list price do it through marketplaces. A marketplace connects buyers with sellers and disclaims everything in between, so when something goes wrong the buyer is left chasing a stranger. People who shop in this category have learned to expect the worst, and the category has given them reasons to.

Valstara works differently. We are the seller, not a listing board. The goods we sell are sourced from verified suppliers or acquired directly, then sold under our own name at our own prices, and we carry full responsibility for fulfilment and support. When something goes wrong, we put it right, because there is nobody else to point at.

What we are, and what we are not

We will not always have the lowest price on the internet. On marketplaces where sellers undercut each other down to nothing, someone will sometimes beat us. What we offer is a price well below list from a seller that states its terms and keeps them.

We sell every product under its provider's own name, so you always know exactly what you are buying. We start with subscriptions for entertainment and for AI tools, and we will add further categories over time. A category goes on sale only when we can state its delivery time and a warranty we are able to honour.

We will not rush you. You will not find countdown timers on this site, or invented scarcity, or figures inflated to look bigger than they are. When we tell you where you stand on the waitlist, it is the real figure from our records.

The name

Val comes from the Latin valere, to be worth. It is the root of value, and everything we do is about worth, meaning real goods well below list price and the assurance that what you bought is what you get.

The star sits inside the name, VAL·STAR·A. It is the symbol people already look for when deciding whether to trust a shop, and it is what travellers once steered by when nothing else could be relied on. Valstara is meant to be that fixed point, the mark you steer by to find real value.

Erasure and retained award records

Once you confirm an erasure request, we stop processing your waitlist record at once. Erasure normally completes immediately and always within one month. We keep the original subscriber record only while completing the deletion of your Brevo contact, then remove your email address and other personal fields from live data, including if you are a winner, apart from the retained award records described here. If you reached the award stage, we keep the award and candidate records unchanged for six years. These include country of residence, age confirmation, check outcomes, substitution choices, values and timestamps. They contain only an opaque subscriber ID as your reference, never your email address. We remove the information that links that ID back to you in live data outside these records.

We retain those award records for sanctions-check obligations under GDPR Article 17(3)(b) and for legal claims under Article 17(3)(e). This exception does not allow us to keep your email address.

Existing encrypted backups are kept beyond use and expire within 30 days. We do not modify individual backups. Before a restored database returns to service, we re-apply erasures from our newest available erasure ledger. That ledger contains only an opaque subscriber ID and the erasure date.

We delete your Brevo contact, rather than just removing it from a mailing list. Erasure is complete only after Brevo confirms deletion or confirms that the contact is absent and we complete local erasure. While the request is pending, we stop email, contact sync, member-page access and leaderboard and giveaway participation. Resend and Brevo keep delivery logs for a limited period under their processor retention. We do not make a separate deletion call to Resend. These delivery logs and the encrypted backups are the only additional retention exceptions.

Erasure also removes the suppression record for your address. If you sign up again, you must confirm your email address again.

Erasure preserves anonymised referral counts. Before the draw snapshot is committed, your entries are voided and excluded. After commitment, we record the erasure separately so the original draw can still be checked. If you are a provisional winner, the prize moves to the next available reserve. A confirmed or delivered award is not reassigned.

You can request a copy of your data or request erasure. We email a confirmation link to prove control of your address. For a question about your data, write to support@valstara.com.

1. Who runs this giveaway

The giveaway is run by 3NITY DIGITAL LIMITED ("we", "us"), a company registered in Hong Kong under Business Registration No. 79886556, at Unit B, 12/F, Hang Seng Causeway Bay Building, 28 Yee Wo Street, Causeway Bay, Hong Kong.

2. Who can enter

You can enter if you are 18 or older when you enter. You can enter from any country, subject to section 9.

You can't enter if you are an employee or contractor of ours, or of anyone involved in running this giveaway, or a member of their immediate family or household.

One registration per person. Creating or controlling more than one registration, including with different email addresses, is not allowed (see section 11).

3. How to enter — no purchase necessary

No purchase or payment is necessary to enter or to win. Buying something will not improve your chances.

  • Join the Valstara waitlist and verify your email address: 1 entry.
  • Each person you refer who joins and verifies their own email address: 1 more entry for you.

You don't need to refer anyone, and you don't need to agree to marketing emails, to enter. A person who joins directly receives exactly the same entry as anyone else, and every entry counts the same in the draw.

Only verified entries count. Referral entries count only for real, separate people who verify their own address, not for addresses you control.

4. Entry period

Entries open at 00:00 UTC on 1 October 2026, or when the Valstara waitlist goes live if that is later. They close at 23:59:59 UTC on 1 March 2027.

To count, your email verification, and the verification of anyone you referred, must be completed before entries close.

We will only change these dates if something outside our control makes it unavoidable, and never in a way that disadvantages people who have already entered.

5. Prizes

There are 5 prizes. Each prize is a Valstara bundle of digital goods with an approximate retail value of €300, making €1,500 in total. Values are stated in euros.

The goods in the bundle will be listed on this page before the draw. No person can win more than one prize.

Prizes are delivered electronically, at no cost to you.

6. If a product isn't available

The goods we carry change over time, so a product in the bundle may no longer be available when prizes are awarded, or may not be available in your region. If that happens:

  1. You choose replacement goods from what we offer, so that your bundle keeps an approximate retail value of at least €300.
  2. If you don't want any replacement, we pay you the approximate retail value of the product that couldn't be supplied, in cash, as a last resort.

We never replace a product just to reduce what the prize costs us.

7. Your chances of winning

Your chances depend on how many eligible entries there are when entries close, so the exact odds can't be known in advance.

Every eligible entry counts equally. Your chance of being drawn is the number of eligible entries you hold divided by the total number of eligible entries. For example, if you hold 3 entries and there are 30,000 eligible entries in total, your chance of being drawn first is 3 in 30,000 (1 in 10,000).

Once a person is drawn, their remaining entries are taken out of the draw, because no one can win more than one prize.

8. How winners are chosen

Winners are drawn on 1 April 2027 by a computer process that selects entries at random, in proportion to the number of eligible entries each person holds.

  • After entries close, we record a complete copy of the eligible entries and a cryptographic fingerprint of it. The random seed is generated only after that fingerprint is recorded, so the entry list can't be changed to suit a particular result.
  • The same process selects the 5 winners and an ordered list of reserves, all at once.
  • The draw can be re-run from these records and produces the same result. We don't announce any result that fails that check.
  • Before any prize is awarded, a person manually reviews the entrants with the most entries for breaches of these rules.

9. Winner checks

We email each provisional winner at their verified address within 7 days of the draw. To receive the prize, you must reply within 14 days of our email and:

  • confirm you are 18 or older;
  • tell us your country of residence (we don't need your postal address; prizes are delivered electronically);
  • if you live in Canada, correctly answer a short mathematical skill-testing question.

We can't award a prize where doing so would break sanctions laws that apply to us, our payment provider or our delivery partners.

If a provisional winner doesn't reply in time, doesn't meet these rules, or can't receive a prize for the reason above, the prize goes to the next reserve from the same draw. We never hold a new draw.

10. Taxes

Winners are responsible for any taxes, duties or charges due on the prize in their country.

11. Disqualification

We may remove entries, or disqualify an entrant, for:

  • creating or controlling more than one registration;
  • referring yourself, or referring addresses that aren't real, separate people;
  • using automated or scripted signups;
  • giving false information, including about your age;
  • interfering with the giveaway or the draw.

Our systems may flag unusual activity, but a person reviews every case before anyone is disqualified. If you are disqualified, you can't win a prize and the entries you earned from referrals are removed. The entries of people you referred are not affected.

If you think we got it wrong, email support@valstara.com within 14 days of our decision and a person will review it again.

12. Winner publicity

We may publish the number of prizes awarded and each winner's first name and surname initial, to show the prizes were genuinely awarded. You can ask us not to publish your name.

We'll only use a winner's photo, testimonial or likeness with separate permission, and giving it is never a condition of receiving a prize.

13. Your data

We use your email address and entries to run the waitlist and this giveaway, as described in our Privacy Notice. Entering does not sign you up for marketing emails. That is a separate, optional choice you can change at any time from your status page.

14. Changes to these rules

We will only change these rules to correct or clarify them, or when something outside our control makes it necessary, and never in a way that disadvantages people who have already entered.

Every change is published as a new version of this page, and your entries stay under the version that applied when you earned them.

15. Governing law

These rules are governed by the laws of Hong Kong. This doesn't affect any rights you have under the mandatory consumer-protection laws of the country where you live.
