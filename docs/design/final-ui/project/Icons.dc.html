Icon sheet

One stroke family: 24-unit grid, 1.75 stroke, round caps and joins, drawn at 16, 20 and 24 (never scaled between). Icons inherit currentColor; inside a coral-tint tile they stay --ink. Every icon is decorative (aria-hidden) and always sits beside a visible word, except the theme toggle, which has an aria-label. Seven additions over the wireframes are tagged.

Sizes

16 inline with 14/20 and 13/18 text and in pills. 20 in buttons, notices, field errors and form fields. 24 in tiles (40 tile; 48 tile at /verify, /me, task pages).

Colour

currentColor everywhere. Tiles: --coral-tint ground, --ink glyph; success tiles --success-tint with --success. Success check-circle uses --success in light and --ink in dark. Field error alert-circle uses --error.

Delivery

One inline SVG sprite (icons.svg, under 6 KB) referenced with <use>. No icon font, no third-party set, no runtime load.

Star accent

Not an icon: a four-point brand star. Coral 12 × 18 before the positioning line; coral-lift 16 × 24 in the guarantee band. Nowhere else.
