# Page design — E11.5 engineering reference

The [development plan](../plan/development-plan.md#epic-e115--frontend-design) governs each ticket. This page records the shared implementation rules for the redesign; it does not change behaviour or the locked palette and type scale.

## Grid and rhythm

Use the mobile-first container: `--container-max: 75rem`, `--gutter` below 48rem and `--gutter-wide` from 48rem. Content follows one container grid; use `data-design-container` on the container and `data-grid-align="start"` or `"end"` on items whose edge must line up. Sections sit on the page ground with `--section-gap` (64px) below 64rem and `--section-gap-wide` (80px) from 64rem. Long prose has `--measure: 68ch`; it sits within the wider composition rather than creating a narrow centred page.

The closed breakpoints are **22.5rem, 48rem, 64rem and 80rem** (E11.5-11a): small is below 48rem, medium is 48rem to below 64rem, large is 64rem and up, and wide is 80rem and up. `22.5rem` is used by the header's Join button and by the landing hero's tight logo strip, both drawn in the 320 frame (E11.5-11c, founder-authorised 2026-10-27). Test at 320, 360, 390, 768, 1024, 1280, 1440 and 1920 CSS pixels, in light and dark themes. The CI literal scan rejects other media-query lengths and values embedded in any `src/**/*.astro` style, including inline Astro style expressions.

## Depth, type and icons

`--shadow-1` is for cards at rest; `--shadow-2` is for the raised hero visual and hovered interactive card; `--shadow-sticky` (plus the dark-only `--edge`) is for the mobile sticky signup bar (E11.5-11h); `--shadow-3` is for overlays. Use the dark versions on dark surfaces. `--on-midnight` colours all text and icons on `midnight`. `coral` on `midnight` is reserved for large text and accents. No new colour or type-size token is introduced.

Use the existing §6.1 type sizes with weight and tracking tokens. Headings and hero copy are left-aligned. `src/components/icon.astro` is a closed inline-SVG registry with token sizes, `currentColor` and decorative hiding by default. The four-point star is a decorative accent only; never arrange three as a rating row. No icon font or remote asset is loaded.

## Shell and primitives (E11.5-03)

`src/layouts/BaseLayout.astro` is the one shell. Trust and legal pages render it through `src/layouts/trust-page.astro` with `themeToggle={false}`, because they ship no client JavaScript beyond the page-view emitter.

- **Skip link**: "Skip to content", the first focusable element, targeting `#main` (E11.5-11b). It sits above the viewport until focused, then shows at the gutter, 8px from the top, on `--surface` with `--ink` text, `--shadow-2`, the edge and the coral focus outline (E11.5-11k). The reveal is instant.
- **Header** (E11.5-11k, from `Header.dc.html`), not sticky, with no menu and no script. Large: 80px tall, items centred with 40px gaps: the wordmark (20/28 600, `--tracking-tight`), the section links (How it works · What you get · Guarantee · FAQ) and, on the right, 12px apart, the theme toggle and Join. Medium: the same with 24px gaps. Small: 72px tall with 8px gaps, the wordmark, the toggle and Join, with no section links. The links sit in a surface capsule (`--shadow-1`, the edge, radius 12, padding 4). Each link is 14/20 500 `ink-600`, 36px tall (`--space-2` + `--space-2-5`), padded 12px, 2px apart at large and touching at medium, radius 8. Hover fills `--wash` and pressing fills `--wash-strong`, both with `--ink` text and no underline; only the fill and the text colour transition, over `--duration-fast`, and neither runs under reduced motion. Focus is the 2px coral outline. **Join is primary only on the landing page.** Every other page draws it secondary, because that page's one primary is its own action. Join is 44px tall with a fixed width at each size, so the right-aligned actions do not move when the font swaps (E2-02): 160px (`--space-10` × 2), and 130px (`--space-2-5` × 6.5) with 14/20 text and 12px padding below `22.5rem`. The secondary keeps its own label size: 14/20 padded 16px below 48rem, 16/24 padded 20px from 48rem. The links point at `#…` on the landing page and `/#…` elsewhere. `/legit`, `/guarantee` and `/about` have no theme toggle. **At 768 the canvas row is 5.4px wider than its 704px content track where the toggle shows.** The layout checks hold the row on the grid (design rule 8), so the wordmark and capsule stay where the canvas draws them, Join ends on the container edge, and the gap between the capsule and the toggle is 18.6px rather than 24px. It reaches 24px at about 774px wide. The founder accepted this on 2026-11-05. The header and footer take no entrance and no scroll reveal.
- **Footer** (E11.5-11k, from `Footer.dc.html`): a full-width `midnight` band with `--on-midnight` text, 64px top and 48px bottom padding, the section gap above it, and `--shadow-band` (none in light; the 1px edge and the cast shadow in dark). `main` has no bottom padding, so on every page the section gap is the only space between the content and the footer; the landing page offsets only main's top padding. Large, on the 12-column grid: the wordmark in columns 1–4, Company (About, Legit, Guarantee) in 5–6, Legal (Terms, Privacy, Storage inventory, Giveaway rules) in 7–8 and Contact (`support@valstara.com`, a `mailto:` link) in 9–12. Column headings are 14/20 600 with 8px below. Links are 14/20 `--on-midnight-600`, 32px tall at large and 44px below it, at least 44px wide. Hover moves the colour to `--on-midnight` and adds the 2px `--coral-lift` underline; the colour and the underline colour transition over `--duration-fast` (the `Tokens.dc.html` motion table; neither runs under reduced motion). Focus is the 2px `--coral-lift` outline and `--on-midnight` text. Medium: the wordmark on its own row, then three span-4 columns with a 32px row gap. Small: the wordmark, then the 4-column grid with 24px row gaps, Company and Legal span 2 and Contact spans 4. The Company and Legal columns share one `nav` laid out with `subgrid`. The footer wordmark keeps a 44px target; its box starts 8px above its text so the text sits on the padding. Below the columns, 48px down (32px small), a 1px `--on-midnight-line` rule, then 24px of padding, then the trademark line and the §3.4 legal line: 14/20 `--on-midnight-600`, 8px apart, at most 760px wide (`--space-10` × 9.5). No payment logos, social links or cookie banner. It stays static, so the sticky bar's footer detection is unaffected.
- **Theme toggle** (restyled by E11.5-11j): a 44px icon button with the inset line and `--shadow-1`, a moon offering dark and a sun offering light. The label stays in the accessible name and the status is still announced; storage key, behaviour and the no-flash bootstrap are unchanged.

## The wireframes (E11.5-11a to E11.5-11h)

The founder's mid-fidelity wireframes are the canvas "Valstara waitlist wireframes" at `https://claude.ai/artifact/UpBYawiwLUpinVSYfBQneJ`, pinned to version `1792974724-d356` (founder-authorised 2026-10-26). They bind the landing page, its signup form and the mobile sticky bar. Where a wireframe and an earlier E11.5 ticket's layout differ, the wireframe wins; behaviour is unchanged. The development plan's wireframe rules govern each ticket. In short:

- The blue annotation layer is never built.
- The wireframe's CSS values are the specification. Every value goes through `tokens.css`.
- Copy is verbatim. Values marked `{dyn}` come from their source computation.
- Placeholders (the dashed logo square, the hard-coded provider list, the grey security-check box, placeholder links) are replaced with the real logos, `src/content/provider-showcase.json`, the Turnstile widget and the permanent routes.
- Fonts stay self-hosted.
- Breakpoints map as follows: the 390 frame is small (below 48rem), the 768 frame is medium (48rem to below 64rem), and the 1440 frame is large (64rem and up). Each layout is fluid between its breakpoints, and the container caps at 1200px (1264px with its 32px gutters).
- Geometry is asserted at 1440, 768 and 390 px, and at 320 where drawn, with a 1px tolerance, in both themes.
- Where artboards disagree on a primitive's size or style, `Components.dc.html` wins; page and state artboards govern placement and layout (founder-authorised 2026-10-29).

**Layout values.** Sections are 80px apart at large widths and 64px at medium and small. Gutters are 32px at medium and large and 16px at small. Medium and large use a 12-column grid with 24px gaps (`--grid-columns-wide`, `--grid-gap-wide`); small uses a 4-column grid with 16px gaps. The header is 80px tall at medium and large and 72px at small.

**Primary is reserved for "Join the waitlist" on the landing page** (founder-authorised 2026-10-26): the header, the hero, the final call to action and the sticky bar. Off the landing page the header Join is secondary (E11.5-11k). Other pages keep their single primary action from E11.5-07 and E11.5-10, such as `/verify`'s confirm button, the task pages' actions and the 404 and 500 home links, and take the new primary styling.

**Recorded deviation from the component sheet** (the final design adopts it, so it stands): the input placeholder is `ink-600`, not the drawn `ink-300`, because `ink-300` is 2.60:1 on surface in light and fails WCAG 1.4.3. Dark `ink-600` is the drawn `#A0A0A8`. No contrast exception is recorded.

**Icons.** `src/components/icon.astro` holds every icon the wireframes draw, transcribed as 24-unit stroke icons (stroke 1.75, round caps and joins): check, link, shield, shield-check, mail, check-circle, upload-share, building, office, people, person, gift, clock, arrow-right, arrow-up, chevron-down, search-check, moon, sun, info, alert-circle, refresh and pause. They render at `size="sm"` (16px), `"base"` (20px) or `"md"` (24px); `"lg"` (40px) remains. The four-point star is the wireframe's 16 × 24 path at `sm` (12 × 18) or `md` (16 × 24), coloured by its parent: coral at 12 × 18 and `coral-lift` at 16 × 24. The E11.5-03 triangle `alert` stays for the task pages.

- **Precedence:** the standing rules, design rules and LOCKED brand values; then the ticket's Build; then the canvas for composition, treatment, geometry and states; then the earlier E11.5 tickets for behaviour and anything the canvas does not draw.
- **Not built from the canvas:** the blue annotation layer, the Proposals board, the copy-proposals table, every numbered image placeholder and its tokens, and the hero's right-hand visual, which the founder will supply.

## Trust, legal and rules pages (E11.5-09, final design E11.5-11s)

`/legit`, `/guarantee`, `/about`, `/terms`, `/privacy`, `/privacy/storage`, `/giveaway/rules` and every `/giveaway/rules/<rules_version>` share `src/layouts/document-page.astro`, rendered inside `trust-page.astro` (trust and legal) or `giveaway-rules-page.astro` (rules). They follow `Doc.dc.html` as `TrustLegit.dc.html`, `TrustGuarantee.dc.html`, `TrustAbout.dc.html`, `LegalPrivacy.dc.html`, `LegalTermsStorage.dc.html`, `Rules.dc.html` and `RulesArchived.dc.html` place it. **No content file is edited, and the rules article stays byte-identical to its recorded hash.** Everything the layout adds sits outside the content article, and the styling is CSS only. Every value is a token or a `calc()` of tokens.

- **Header band** (founder decisions 2026-10-23). The band markup is an eyebrow from `src/copy/document-pages.ts` ("Trust", "Legal" or "Giveaway rules"), placed immediately before the content article, and the summary line, placed immediately after it. The content's own leading H1 is the page's only H1. The article is `display: contents` inside one grid, so the eyebrow, the H1 and the summary take its first three rows, and the rest of the content flows below in source order. Some older Safari and Chromium releases drop the landmark role of a `display: contents` element; the founder accepted this on 2026-10-24, because the H1 cannot move and the article markup cannot change. Nothing in the content is hidden. The summary is the content file's frontmatter `description`, or the revision's `description` on rules pages. Legal pages use their existing `src/copy/legal.ts` description, because their content files have no description.
- **The band's finish** (E11.5-11s). The band starts at the header (it takes back the shell's top padding) and is a full-bleed `surface` paint behind the first three rows: the grid's `::before`, bled to both viewport edges by a `--bleed` spread shadow and clipped to its own height, so it never widens the page. The eyebrow is 14/20 600 `ink-600` after a 16 × 2 `coral` rule, with 40px above it (64px from 48rem). The H1 is 32/40 700 and the summary 16/24 `ink-600`, 12px apart, the band text at most 47rem wide. The summary has 40px under it (48px from 48rem), and the body starts 40px further down (48px from 48rem, 64px from 64rem). On `/guarantee` the band is `midnight`, with `--on-midnight` and `--on-midnight-600` text and a `coral-lift` rule, and the three promise headings carry the band's weight: 32/40 700 under a 2px `ink` rule, each lead paragraph 20/28 500. On `/about` the canvas's founder photo (`IMG-01`) is not built (final-design rule 3), so the band text spans the full width, as the image manifest's fallback specifies.
- **Columns.** Single column below 64rem, where the side card renders as a block 40px after the prose, not sticky. From 64rem, the prose runs up to `--measure` beside a sticky side card (24px from the top), 80px away, which takes the remaining width, never narrower than `--space-10 × 4`, and starts level with the body. The card is the component library's side card. On trust pages it summarises the Triple Guarantee: the numerals (in 32px `--ring-numeral` rings, hidden from assistive technology, because the list is ordered) and titles are the approved `landingCopy.trust.promises`, 12px apart under hairlines, then a text link with an arrow to `/guarantee` (left out on `/guarantee` itself) and a full-width Join button to `/#join`. On legal and rules pages it is a contents list ("On this page") of the `h2` anchors already in the rendered content (`src/server/content/contents.ts`): 14/20 `ink-600` capsule links, 44px tall below 64rem and 36px from it. No list renders where there are none (`/terms`, `/privacy/storage`).
- **Archived revision notice.** A superseded rules revision keeps its archived notice above the band, outside the hashed article: a full-bleed `coral-tint` strip (the same `--bleed` paint), 16px top and bottom, 14/20 500 `ink` beside the 20px `history` icon. It is calm, not an alarm.
- **Join bar.** On the three trust pages only, below 48rem: a fixed full-width primary link to `/#join` on `surface` with `--shadow-sticky`, padded 12px and the gutter (and at the bottom the safe area or 12px, whichever is larger), with no form and no script. The page reserves its height with `scroll-padding-block-end` and the footer's bottom padding, so it never covers content or a focused element.
- **Prose.** 16/24 text, paragraphs 16px apart. An H2 is 24/32 600, 48px above its text, 12px over what follows; an H3 is 20/28 600, 32px above. Bullets are 6px `coral` dots, numbered items 14px mono `ink-600` figures. Links are 500 `ink` with the 2px `coral` underline; below 48rem each is at least `--tap-min` each way, and from 48rem they are inline text again. Blockquotes sit on `--wash`, inline code on `--wash-strong`.
- **Tables.** A table sits on a `surface` card with `--inset-line` and `--shadow-1`, its header row on `--well`. Each table is its own grid, with subgrid row groups and rows, and its own horizontal scroll container, so it can never widen the page. Cells wrap long words (`overflow-wrap: anywhere`), so at every test width the tables fit rather than scroll: a scroll region with nothing focusable inside cannot be reached by keyboard (axe `scrollable-region-focusable`), and the content markup cannot be given a `tabindex`. A key-value table (the content gives it an empty header row) runs 38% / the rest, with its empty header row at no height, not hidden. Below 48rem the text is 13/18, and a table of three or more columns restacks each row, CSS only: the first cell across the row, the second and third side by side beneath it, and a fourth on its own line.
- **Motion** (E11.5-11s). It runs only with motion allowed. Otherwise everything is at rest and anchors jump.
  - **The article never animates**, so reading is never disturbed: no element inside it carries an animation or a transition, in any state. Motion applies only to elements outside it, by CSS, so the hashed markup stays byte-identical.
  - **The band and the side card enter at first paint** on the motion system's entrance: the eyebrow (index 0) and the summary (index 2) rise `--enter-distance` over `--duration-enter`. The title, index 1, is the content's H1, inside the article, so it does not move. The side card follows at index 3 with `data-enter="shift"`. As on `/verify` and `/me`, nothing fades (`shift` only), so every word, the Join button and the contents links are at full opacity, readable and usable from first paint. All of it ends by 580ms.
  - **Contents links** take the shell's link motion (E11.5-11k): on hover the text turns `ink`, the capsule fills with `--wash` and the `coral` underline comes in, over `--duration-fast`. Heading anchors scroll smoothly (E11.5-11i) and stop 24px under the viewport top.
  - **The join bar** slides up from below its own height over `--duration-base` on load, as the sticky bar does.

`test/document-pages-rendered.test.mjs` asserts the unchanged content and rules hashes, one H1, the band order, no hidden content element, zero JavaScript, the contents anchors, the table fit, the join bar, the layout checks and axe. `test/document-pages-final-design-rendered.test.mjs` asserts the canvas geometry at each drawn width in both themes at rest, the table restack and the 320px fit, the entrance and its reduced-motion stop, no motion inside the article at first paint or under hover and focus, and the smooth contents anchors.

## Task pages and error pages (E11.5-10)

`/unsubscribe`, `/dsr` (request and confirmation) and `/giveaway/award` (every state) share `src/layouts/task-card.astro`. It is zero-JS: a Container holding a left-aligned Card capped at `--measure`, a state icon beside the page's only H1, then one line, any form and the actions. The card carries `data-task-state`, and its slot content gets these classes:

- `task-card__actions`: one primary Button first, then at most one secondary Button. On `/unsubscribe` the primary action unsubscribes from the message and leaving the waitlist is the secondary. On the substitution form the primary action chooses replacement goods and the cash decline is the secondary. Both are one click.

## Motion (E11.5-11i)

Design rule 12, as amended on 2026-10-30 (founder-authorised): motion makes the site feel alive, but it never carries meaning on its own, never delays an action and never moves text while someone is reading it. It is CSS only, built from the tokens and utilities below. There are four kinds, and **nothing else moves**:

- **State transitions:** hover, press, focus, expand, select and the sticky bar's reveal, over `--duration-fast` or `--duration-base` with `--ease-out`. The canvas's "Motion · state changes only" table binds every transition it lists.
- **First-paint entrances:** once per page load, at most 600ms including the stagger. The H1 and every form control are at full opacity and usable from first paint; they only translate.
- **Scroll reveals:** below-the-fold content rises and fades in as it enters the viewport. A browser without scroll-driven animations shows the content at rest. Revealed content is at rest by the time its top reaches 70% of the viewport height, and focused content is always at rest.
- **Page transitions:** a cross-fade between same-origin pages (cross-document view transitions), and the theme toggle's cross-fade.

Never: parallax, bounce or overshoot, looping or ambient animation (the in-flight spinner excepted), auto-advancing carousels or marquees, animated counters or figures, typewriter text, cursor-following effects, scroll-jacking, or a script or library for motion. Motion animates `opacity`, transforms and the stroke drawing of decorative SVG, never a property that moves layout, except a disclosure the visitor opens. **Under `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` all of it stops:** content renders at rest, nothing enters, pages swap instantly and anchors jump.

**Tokens** (in `tokens.css`, on bare `:root` only, because motion does not change with the theme; listed in [tokens.md](tokens.md#motion-tokens-e115-11i)): `--duration-fast` 150ms and `--duration-base` 200ms for state transitions, with `--ease-out`; `--duration-enter` 400ms for one element's entrance or reveal; `--stagger` 60ms between items that enter together; `--duration-page` 200ms for the page and theme cross-fade; `--ease-enter`, a curve with no overshoot; `--reveal-distance` 1.5rem and `--enter-distance` 0.75rem; `--lift` (the 2px card lift) and `--press-shift` (the 1px button press); `--reveal-range` and `--reveal-step` for scroll reveals. They are starting values. The founder reviews them in motion at E11.5-12, and a change is a token edit. `--reveal-range` and `--reveal-step` were tuned in E11.5-11i from `entry 0% cover 20%` and `4%`, because a short element at the very end of a page, or on a page too short to scroll, never reached rest with those values.

**Utilities** live in `src/styles/motion.css`, a global stylesheet `BaseLayout` loads after `tokens.css`. Every rule in it sits inside `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: no-preference)`. Each start state exists only as an animation's first keyframe, and no keyframe names an end, so every animation ends on the element's own resting style, and a browser that runs none of it shows the content at rest.

| Utility | Use | Behaviour |
|---|---|---|
| `data-enter` | First-paint entrance | Fades in and rises by `--enter-distance` over `--duration-enter` with `--ease-enter`. `style="--enter-index: n"` starts it `n × --stagger` later. The index is capped at 3, so an entrance ends within 580ms. |
| `data-enter="shift"` | The H1 and form controls | Rises into place only, and never changes opacity: visible and usable from first paint. |
| `data-enter="fade"` | Content inside an element that already rises (E11.5-11l) | Fades in only, so it travels exactly its container's distance. |
| `data-reveal` | Below-the-fold content | Scroll-driven (`animation-timeline: view(block 0)`, over `--reveal-range`; the zero inset ignores the root's scroll padding, which reserves the mobile sticky bar while it shows, so the 70% guarantee holds with the bar on screen, E11.5-11n), inside `@supports (animation-timeline: view())`. Rises by `--reveal-distance` and fades in. Siblings with `style="--reveal-index: n"` start `n × --reveal-step` later in the range and come to rest at the same end, so a row reveals in reading order. The index is capped at 4, because each step is a fifth of the range; later items start with index 4. |
| Focus | Any entering or revealed element | Puts it at rest at once. A reveal takes this from visible focus only (`:is(:focus-visible, :has(:focus-visible))`): keyboard focus, or focus moved by script, on the element or inside it. A mouse press focuses a link or button without showing focus, so the reveal holds still under the pointer and the click lands; snapping it to rest on the press moved the target before the release (E11.5-11p, founder decision 2026-11-10). An entrance takes it from `:focus-within`. An entrance is ended (zero duration and delay) rather than removed, so once it has run its course it does not start again when focus leaves (E11.5-11l). The end lasts only while focus stays: if focus leaves before the entrance would have ended, the browser resumes it. So the signup island also removes `data-enter` from each element focus enters, and those entrances stay ended. Without JavaScript only the CSS end applies. The other hero entrances hold nothing focusable. |
| Page transitions | Every page | `@view-transition { navigation: auto; }`, with the root cross-fade over `--duration-page`. |
| Smooth anchors | Every page | `scroll-behavior: smooth` on the root. |

No production page applies `data-enter` or `data-reveal` until the ticket that redesigns it does (E11.5-11j to E11.5-11t). Page transitions, smooth scrolling and the theme cross-fade apply site-wide. `ThemeToggle` wraps its theme switch in `document.startViewTransition()` where that API exists and reduced motion is not requested, so `data-theme` changes in the transition's update a frame after the click; with reduced motion, or without the API, it changes synchronously, as before. The storage key, the no-flash script and the labels are unchanged. With smooth anchor scrolling, the signup island moves focus to the email field (for a `data-focus-signup` link) once the scroll ends, with `preventScroll`.

**Guards** ship with the system:

- **The literal-value scan** (`scripts/ci/literal-values.mjs`) covers every `.astro` file under `src/` and every stylesheet under `src/styles/`, at any depth, except `tokens.css`. Beyond the E11.5-01 properties, it rejects a literal duration or easing in the `transition` and `animation` shorthands, in `*-delay` and in `*-timing-function` (`linear`, the spinner's constant turn, is the one keyword easing allowed); a literal length or percentage in `animation-range*`; a literal length or percentage in `translate`, a literal factor in `scale` and a literal angle in `rotate`, other than their identity values; a literal length inside `transform`; and a literal length, duration or easing function in any declaration inside `@keyframes`. Angles, percentages and unitless numbers inside `transform` remain allowed (a 180° chevron turn, a bar sliding in from 100%), as does a unitless multiplier in a `translate` `calc()`.
- **The motion-property scan** (`scripts/ci/motion-properties.mjs`): a keyframe in `src/` animates only `opacity`, `transform`, `translate`, `scale`, `rotate` and `stroke-dashoffset`, and a transition (longhand or shorthand) that names a layout property (`width`, `height` and their logical, `min-` and `max-` forms, `inset*`, `top`, `right`, `bottom`, `left`, `margin*`, `padding*`, or `all`, including a shorthand item that names no property, which is an implicit `all`) fails, except where every selector of the rule ends on `::details-content`. Both scans run in `npm run security`.
- **Reduced motion** (`test/accessibility.test.mjs`): on every rendered route, nothing has a live transition or animation, no element has a scroll-driven animation, `document.getAnimations()` is empty once the page has loaded, and a same-origin navigation starts no view transition.
- **The motion fixture** (`test/motion-system.test.mjs`) builds a page from the shipping `tokens.css` and `motion.css`. With animations disabled, or without scroll-driven support, every reveal and entrance element is at rest. Every revealed element is at rest once its top is at 70% of the viewport height, and at the page end, at every test width. Each one has the same box at rest as under reduced motion, within 1px.
- **Focus is never mid-motion** (founder decisions 2026-10-31; `test/helpers/motion.mjs`, run by the E11.5-11h focus walk and proven on planted fixtures): no entrance, reveal or other animation is in effect on the focused element or any ancestor. For a reveal this applies to visible focus, the focus the walk moves with the keyboard: a mouse press leaves a reveal where it is, so the click lands (E11.5-11p, founder decision 2026-11-10). The only motion allowed in progress is a transition the focus itself starts, and it must end within `--duration-base`; the check lets it finish, bounded by that duration. Then each element is at full opacity with no displacing transform. A native checkbox or radio is a transparent 44px target over the box it draws, so its own opacity is exempt; its drawn box must be at full opacity with a visible focus ring.

**At-rest measurement.** By rule 12 the reduced-motion layout is the at-rest layout. `checkPageLayout`, `checkShellLayout` and `checkRegionLayout` measure with reduced motion emulated and then restore the page's own preference. `assertEnforcedPages`, `assertShellLayout` and the contact sheet open reduced-motion contexts. Geometry tests from E11.5-11j onward measure at rest the same way. Because smooth scrolling also applies to programmatic and focus scrolls, a test's setup scrolls use `behavior: 'instant'`, and a test that reads a position after Tab or an anchor jump waits, bounded, for the scroll to end (`scrollSettled` in `test/helpers/motion.mjs`, founder decision 2026-10-31).

**Recordings.** A PR that adds motion attaches two short screen recordings of the surfaces it touched, one at 390px and one at 1440px, both with motion on, made with Playwright's local video capture. They are not committed and load nothing. The contact sheet is still captured at rest.

**Waitlist deviation from identity-guidelines §7 "Motion"** (DIRECTIONAL: "150–200ms ease-out on state changes"): the waitlist adds the first-paint entrance, scroll reveals and page transitions. It keeps no bounce, no parallax and no auto-advancing carousel.
