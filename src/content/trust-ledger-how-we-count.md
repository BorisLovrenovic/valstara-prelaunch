## How we count

These definitions are the same every month. If we ever change one, that month's entry says what changed and why.

Every figure is an exact count. A rounded figure is labelled as one. Months are calendar months in UTC. We publish an entry every month, and if we ever miss one, the next entry says so. We never edit an entry once it is published. If we get something wrong, the next entry corrects it, with the date.

### Waitlist

- **Confirmed members:** people who joined the waitlist and confirmed their email address. Anyone who has not confirmed is not counted.
- **Total:** confirmed members on the day we prepare the entry.
- **New this month:** members who confirmed during the month.
- **Joined through a referral:** the share of this month's new members who joined through another member's or a partner's link or code.

### Suppliers

A supplier is a business we buy goods from to fulfil your orders. We check every supplier before we buy from them for customers. We never name a supplier, publish what a supplier charges us, or say which supplier serves which product. We may use more than one supplier for the same product, so we publish counts only.

- **Evaluated:** a supplier whose checks we have finished, whether they passed or not. A supplier we are still checking is not counted yet.
- **Verified:** a supplier that passed every one of these checks:
  1. **Their history.** We look at how long they have sold these goods online, who they have sold to, including other sellers like us and marketplaces, and what their buyers say about them.
  2. **Delivery.** We make a test purchase. It must arrive within 24 hours.
  3. **A problem order.** We make a second purchase that cannot be linked to the first, with a problem built in on purpose, such as wrong account details, or an account that still has an active subscription. We see how the supplier handles it.
  4. **Claims.** We ask for replacements and refunds, and see how the supplier handles them and how quickly.
  5. **The goods keep working.** We check our test purchases regularly: for 30 days on monthly subscriptions, and for 3 months on everything else.
  6. **Terms in writing.** The supplier agrees warranty and replacement terms with us in writing. Their cover can be shorter than the cover we give you. Where it is, we carry the difference ourselves.
- **Rejected:** a supplier that failed a check or would not agree to our terms. We also reject a new supplier with no track record that wants to sell goods we consider high risk.

A supplier rejected for something they can fix, such as slow delivery or slow handling of claims, can be checked again later. A supplier we believe sells goods obtained by fraud is rejected for good. Each supplier is counted once, under their latest result.

### Launch products

- **Launch products:** the providers we currently plan to sell when the store opens. The list changes as products come and go before then, so each entry gives the number on the day we prepare it.
- **With verified supply:** a launch product counts only when every plan we will sell for it comes from at least one verified supplier. A provider with some plans covered and others not is not counted.

### Partner programme

- **Applications received:** applications sent through our partner page, including any we turn down.
- **Approved:** applications we accepted.
- **Active:** partners who have signed their agreement and whose partner code is live on the last day of the month. A paused partner is not active.

### Build progress

Each item is marked done, in progress or next. Done means it is finished and in use.

### Launch status

We say whether early access is on track for September 2027. If that changes, we say so here and give the reason.
