# Cookie and storage inventory

This site has one browser-storage entry. It sets no cookies.

| Name             | Kind          | Purpose                                   |
| ---------------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| `valstara-theme` | Local storage | Remembers your light or dark theme choice |

This preference is functional and does not identify you. The site does not use browser storage for analytics, advertising or referrals. Referral information is handled on the server. The site runs no device-fingerprinting API. Cloudflare Turnstile protects forms from abuse; the rendered-page check verifies that it does not store or access information on your device.

No non-essential information is stored on or accessed from your device, so there is no cookie banner. Three automated checks guard this: one rejects unapproved cookie and storage use, one rejects site-run fingerprinting APIs, and one rejects unapproved third-party requests from pages. Rendered-page checks also inspect what the browser actually loads and stores.

Read the [privacy notice](/privacy) for how we use personal data.
