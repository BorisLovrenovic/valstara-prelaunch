Joining the waitlist and entering the giveaway use the contract basis. Marketing uses a separate, optional, unticked choice. Withdrawing marketing consent never removes your waitlist place or giveaway entries. You can change that choice from your status page or the link in a marketing email. Leaving the waitlist entirely is a separate choice.

We collect no country or other location at signup. We request a provisional winner's country of residence at award time, and a partner applicant's country of residence on the partner application, for the partner contract. Applying to the partner programme uses the same email confirmation as the waitlist: confirming your application also confirms your waitlist place. We do not collect a postal address for the giveaway; prizes are delivered electronically.

Our email providers process message data to deliver mail. Cloudflare processes connection information for site security and the verification challenge. The waitlist database is separate from the storefront database. This draft will be replaced with reviewed details of recipients, transfers and your rights.

## Cookie and storage inventory

This site has two browser-storage entries and sets no cookies:

| Name             | Kind                     | Purpose                                            |
| ---------------- | ------------------------ | -------------------------------------------------- |
| `valstara-theme` | Local storage            | Remembers your light or dark theme choice          |
| `cf.turnstile.u` | Cloudflare local storage | Helps secure a form when its Turnstile widget runs |

The theme choice is functional and does not identify you. Cloudflare Turnstile reads and writes `cf.turnstile.u` only when a protected form's widget runs; it is used to protect the form you submit from abuse. We rely on the Article 5(3) strictly-necessary exemption for that form-security use. The site sets no tracking cookie and uses no advertising tracker. This is why the site has no cookie banner.

The [full cookie and storage inventory](/privacy/storage) explains the no-banner basis and the checks that keep it true.

## Erasure and retained award records

Once you confirm an erasure request, we stop processing your waitlist record at once. Erasure normally completes immediately and always within one month. We keep the original subscriber record only while completing the deletion of your Brevo contact, then remove your email address and other personal fields from live data, including if you are a winner, apart from the retained award records described here. If you reached the award stage, we keep the award and candidate records unchanged for six years. These include country of residence, age confirmation, check outcomes, substitution choices, values and timestamps. They contain only an opaque subscriber ID as your reference, never your email address. We remove the information that links that ID back to you in live data outside these records.

We retain those award records for sanctions-check obligations under GDPR Article 17(3)(b) and for legal claims under Article 17(3)(e). This exception does not allow us to keep your email address.

Existing encrypted backups are kept beyond use and expire within 30 days. We do not modify individual backups. Before a restored database returns to service, we re-apply erasures from our newest available erasure ledger. That ledger contains only an opaque subscriber ID and the erasure date.

We delete your Brevo contact, rather than just removing it from a mailing list. Erasure is complete only after Brevo confirms deletion or confirms that the contact is absent and we complete local erasure. While the request is pending, we stop email, contact sync, member-page access and leaderboard and giveaway participation. Resend and Brevo keep delivery logs for a limited period under their processor retention. We do not make a separate deletion call to Resend. These delivery logs and the encrypted backups are the only additional retention exceptions.

Erasure also removes the suppression record for your address. If you sign up again, you must confirm your email address again.

Erasure preserves anonymised referral counts. If the partner who referred you is erased after you earn your welcome discount and wave-1 place, we keep those benefits in a record for you. It contains no partner identifier, code or link to the original referral record. Before the draw snapshot is committed, your entries are voided and excluded. After commitment, we record the erasure separately so the original draw can still be checked. If you are a provisional winner, the prize moves to the next available reserve. A confirmed or delivered award is not reassigned.

You can [request a copy of your data](/dsr?operation=export) or [request erasure](/dsr?operation=delete). We email a confirmation link to prove control of your address. For a question about your data, write to support@valstara.com.
