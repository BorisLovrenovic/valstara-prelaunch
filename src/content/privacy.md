Joining the waitlist and entering the giveaway use the contract basis. Marketing uses a separate, optional, unticked choice. Withdrawing marketing consent never removes your waitlist place or giveaway entries. You can change that choice from your status page or the link in a marketing email. Leaving the waitlist entirely is a separate choice.

We collect no country or other location at signup. We request only a provisional winner's country of residence at award time. We do not collect a postal address for the giveaway; prizes are delivered electronically.

Our email providers process message data to deliver mail. Cloudflare processes connection information for site security and the verification challenge. The waitlist database is separate from the storefront database. This draft will be replaced with reviewed details of recipients, transfers and your rights.

## Cookie and storage inventory

This site has one browser-storage entry:

| Name             | Kind          | Purpose                                   |
| ---------------- | ------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| `valstara-theme` | Local storage | Remembers your light or dark theme choice |

The theme choice is functional and does not identify you. The site sets no tracking cookie. It uses no advertising tracker. The signup form uses Cloudflare Turnstile to prevent abuse; our site does not add a browser-storage identifier for that purpose. This is why the site has no cookie banner.

For a question about your data or to ask for access or erasure, write to support@valstara.com.
