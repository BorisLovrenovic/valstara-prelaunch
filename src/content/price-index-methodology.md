# How the price index works

We collect official list prices once a month from public price listings, and check them by hand against providers' own pricing pages where needed.

## What a price on our pages is

Each price is the list price of one plan in one market. It is shown in that market's own currency, exactly as it is listed. We never convert a price into another currency.

Beside each price is the date it was last verified: the date the listing last checked it, the date we checked it by hand, or, where the listing gives no date, the day we read it.

## When we publish a price

We publish a price only when it passes every check. It must be in the market's own currency and above zero.

A price that has changed by more than 40% since the last price we published is held back until a person has checked it and approved it.

If a listing and our own hand check disagree, neither price is published until a person decides.

If a plan disappears from a listing, we flag it for a person to check rather than removing it quietly.

Where we have no verified price for a plan, we show none.

## When a price comes off our pages

Each time we update the index, a price we read from a listing more than 45 days earlier, or checked by hand more than 90 days earlier, is marked out of date and taken off our pages until it has been checked again.

## Price changes

We record a price change only when one published price replaces another. Our record of past changes is never edited.

## Comparing markets

Where we compare markets, we rank them by price on a common basis and show each price in its own market's currency.
